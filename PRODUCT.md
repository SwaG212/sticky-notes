# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows users who want to capture scattered thoughts quickly, turn them into actionable tasks, and keep lightweight notes without leaving a compact desktop utility.

## Product Purpose

Sticky Notes combines quick capture, AI-assisted task organization, project views, reminders, a calendar, and a multi-file notepad. Success means users can record, find, prioritize, and complete work with minimal interruption.

## Brand Personality

Calm, compact, and dependable. The interface should feel familiar and efficient, with restrained feedback that keeps attention on the current task.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard styling, attention-seeking motion, unfamiliar replacements for standard desktop controls, and visual changes that make equivalent actions behave inconsistently across views.

## Design Principles

- Keep capture and task completion faster than manual organization.
- Preserve consistent states and motion across equivalent views.
- Reveal advanced controls only where they are useful.
- Prefer familiar desktop interactions and predictable navigation.
- Keep local data and user intent trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, visible disabled and focus states, sufficient text contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives for nonessential animation.
